Why it's great

Fast, verified transfers

Straight over your Wi-Fi at full speed, checked with SHA-256 on both ends. Keeps going with the screen off.

A profile per device

Where every folder goes and exactly what belongs on each handheld. Add to a folder and it comes along.

Safe removals

It knows every file it copied. Removing from a profile removes only those; your saves are never touched.

Compare with device

See what was deleted on the handheld and what isn't there yet, right in your Library.

Artwork and ES-DE

Covers, screenshots and details from libretro, ScreenScraper and Steam, uploaded into ES-DE.

GameNative configs

The best community config for each PC game on your GPU, saved ready to import.

Control it from the couch

Open Odin Sync from the handheld and its browser lands in your Library, signed in.

Plays nice with games

Low impact transfers cap the speed so you can keep playing while it syncs.

Finds your devices

The PC and the app remember each other and reconnect by themselves, even on new IPs.

Up and running in minutes

  1. Install Odin Sync on your PCand set your PIN.
  2. Add your foldersROMs, PC games, music, videos, anything.
  3. Install the app on your handheldfrom the link in Devices; allow it to access files.
  4. Pairtap Find Odin Sync on the PC and enter your PIN.
  5. Pick and synctick what you want in Library and press Sync device.
