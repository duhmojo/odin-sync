Pick your ROMs, PC games, music and videos on the PC. Hit Sync. Odin Sync puts exactly what belongs on each Android handheld, in the right folders, over Wi-Fi, and checks every byte.

Free for Windows, Linux and macOS. The Android app installs from the PC app.

The usual way

  • Drag folders over one at a time, then forget what you copied.
  • Transfers stall the second the handheld's screen goes dark.
  • ADB, cables, developer options and pairing codes that expire.
  • A dropped connection leaves half a game behind.
  • Cleaning up means hunting through folders on a tiny screen.

With Odin Sync

  • Tick what you want once; it remembers, per device.
  • Keeps going with the screen off.
  • No cables, no ADB: a small app and your PIN.
  • Every file is checked with SHA-256 before it replaces anything.
  • Untick it on the PC and it is removed from the handheld, safely.

Up and running in minutes

No developer options, no cables, nothing to configure on the handheld beyond one permission.

  1. Install on your PCand set your PIN.
  2. Add your foldersROMs, PC games, music, videos.
  3. Install the appon your handheld, from the link in Devices.
  4. PairFind Odin Sync on the PC, enter your PIN.
  5. Pick and syncTick what you want, press Sync.

Why it's great

Built by someone who got tired of copying ROMs by hand.

Your library, per device

  • A profile for every handheld: where each folder goes, and what belongs on it.
  • Check a whole system and new games come along by themselves.
  • Compare with device shows what was deleted there and what isn't synced yet.

Know before you sync

  • A clear review: what's new, what changed, what goes away.
  • Free space checked before a single byte moves.
  • Safe removals: only files Odin Sync copied, never your saves.

The handheld's storage, from your desk

  • Browse internal storage and SD cards with real folder sizes.
  • Upload and download files and whole folders.
  • Finds the device by itself, even when its IP changes.
